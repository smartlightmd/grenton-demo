Scenes

A day in a Lagmar apartment with Grenton

A scene is several actions in one touch — or automatically, by time, by a sensor or when you get home. Here's what an ordinary day looks like.

  1. 07:00

    Morning

    from Comfort

    Bedroom blinds open, the light slowly brightens, the bathroom is already warm. No alarm clock, no hunting for switches.

    CLT
  2. 08:30

    “Leaving”

    from Basic

    One button by the door: all lights go off, heating switches to eco mode, the sensors keep watching the apartment.

    LTW
  3. 13:00

    During the day

    from Comfort

    The app shows whether everything is off. If the leak sensor trips, the water is shut off and you get a notification.

    WP
  4. 18:30

    “I'm home”

    from Basic

    Press the button or open the smart lock: the hallway light comes on and the heating returns to your comfort temperature.

    LTI
  5. 21:00

    “Movie”

    in Premium

    Lights dim, blinds close, sound starts in the living room — all with one touch of the panel.

    LCA
  6. 23:00

    “Night”

    from Comfort

    Lights go off across the apartment, the bedroom cools down, and a soft night light comes on in the hallway as you walk by.

    LTM
  7. ✈

    “Holiday”

    from Comfort

    Lights and blinds run on a schedule as if someone were home. The video intercom answers through your phone, and you hear about any leak at once.

    LCI

We set the scenes up around your routine when the system is commissioned and change them whenever you ask.
