How it works

Reliable inside — simple to use outside

Grenton is a professional wired system made in Poland. Everything that matters happens in your apartment's distribution board; you only see neat panels and the app.

What the system is made of

The brain — the CLU central unit

Stores and runs every scene right in the apartment. Internet is only needed for remote access — without it the home keeps working.

LTC

The hands — modules in the board

Control lighting, heating, blinds and sockets. They sit in the distribution board, not in the walls, so they're easy to service and extend.

WSMQ

The eyes — sensors

The Multisensor measures temperature, humidity, light and motion; separate sensors watch for leaks and smoke.

The interfaces — panels and the app

Smart Panel and Touch Panel on the wall, the premium Monolith line, the myGrenton app and classic push switches — use whatever suits you.
