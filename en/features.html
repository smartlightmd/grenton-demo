Not gadgets — ready-made comfort in every room

We don't sell “smart bulbs”. The smart home in your apartment ties lighting, heating, blinds, sensors and the intercom into one system — and you run the apartment the way you like: from the wall, from your phone, or without touching anything at all.

Light that adapts to you

The right light in one touch — and no lamp left on.

  • Lighting groups per room and an “All off” button by the front doorfrom Basic
  • Smooth dimming in the living room and bedroomfrom Comfort
  • Night lighting in the hallway and bathroom, triggered by motionfrom Comfort
  • Decorative lighting and “Dinner”, “Guests”, “Reading” scenesin Premium

Heat on schedule, not “just in case”

Comfortable temperature where and when you are — without extra bills.

  • Thermostat and schedule: economical by day, warm when you get homefrom Basic
  • Separate temperature per zone: a cool bedroom, a warm bathroomfrom Comfort
  • Air-conditioner control from the same panel and appfrom Comfort
  • CO₂ and humidity monitoring — it tells you when to air the roomin Premium

Your apartment warns you before the neighbours do

A leak, smoke or an open door — you know at once, wherever you are.

  • Leak sensors in the kitchen and bathroom with phone notificationsfrom Basic
  • A motorised valve shuts the water off on its ownfrom Comfort
  • Smoke sensor with app notification (does not replace the building's fire alarm)from Comfort
  • Alarm-system integration and presence simulation while you're on holidayin Premium

Mornings without an alarm clock

Blinds open on their own — at sunrise or on your schedule.

  • Motorised blinds in the living room and bedroomfrom Comfort
  • Schedules linked to the “Morning” and “Night” scenesfrom Comfort
  • Every window in the apartment — one “Close all” buttonin Premium
